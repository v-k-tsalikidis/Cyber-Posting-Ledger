No non-public, confidential, or proprietary internal data is contained within or processed by this tool.

## Responsible & Ethical Use

- **Local-First & Non-Intrusive:** This tool operates strictly on local data provided by the user. It does not perform automated scraping, aggressive web crawling, credential harvesting, or unauthorized requests against employer career portals.
- **Verification Requirement:** Users are advised to verify every vacancy notice against the official primary source career website of the respective organization prior to taking career decisions or submitting application materials.
